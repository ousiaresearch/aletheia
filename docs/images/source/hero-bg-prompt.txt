USECASE: branded release hero background for a developer product announcement page.
ASSET TYPE: a wide horizontal banner background, 16:9, at least 2560x1440, to be typeset later.

SCENE — an archived mail-order broadside for a printed index-card memory archive.
A fanned stack of printed index cards, bound along one edge, sits on a sheet of washed newsprint
that has been handled for decades: cards slightly rotated against each other, one card pulled a
few degrees out of the pack, a small red stamped tab folded over the top card's edge, a punched
hole and a thin red margin rule on the top card. Thin press column rules cross the newsprint
behind the cards. The paper is creased and scuffed where it was folded twice.

COMPOSITION:
- The card stack occupies the RIGHT 55-60% of the frame and bleeds off the right, top and bottom
  edges, so it reads as an object photographed on a page rather than an illustration.
- The LEFT 40% is quiet washed newsprint: faint column rules, subtle ink mottle, a couple of small
  registration marks. Leave this band EMPTY of objects and heavy texture — real type will be set
  there later, and anything busy will fight it.
- Camera slightly above the page, a shallow perspective, soft even light from the upper left, no
  dramatic spotlight.

STYLE — underground technical-art imprint, analog newspaper direct-response lane:
- printed on newsprint, not rendered: fine halftone dot screen, paper fibers, ink mottle, uneven
  inking, slight plate misregistration, faint scan haze, worn edges.
- 2-4 inks only: newsprint cream #E3DCC7, xerox black #151411, washed gray-green #5F625C, aged tan
  #CDBE9C, with ONE signal accent: coupon red #9B2F24 used only on the stamped tab and the thin
  margin rule.

CONSTRAINTS:
- Generate NO text, letters, numbers, logos, wordmarks, signage, labels, captions or writing of any
  kind anywhere. The index cards and the newsprint must show only abstract non-legible line
  impressions, ruled bars and blank fields — never real characters or numerals.
- No faces, no hands, no figures, no UI, no mockups, no device screens.
- No glossy 3D, no neon or cyberpunk glow, no gradients or lens flare, no stock "AI brain" or
  neural-network lattice, no token or coin imagery, no clean corporate vector look.
